Privacy

What 3D Print Circuitry (3dprintcircuitry.com) collects today. Last updated 21 November 2026.

Who runs this site

3D Print Circuitry is part of the PrintWire network. You can reach PrintWire through the contact page.

What the forms collect

The forms on this site collect only what you type into them: your name, your email address and your message, and your company, your phone number and an attached file if you choose to give them.

How it is used

We use it to answer you.

If you ask for a quote, PrintWire may pass your request to manufacturers to obtain pricing. That can include the details you gave us.

Who processes it

Form submissions are handled by Web3Forms (web3forms.com), which delivers them to PrintWire by email. Web3Forms acts as our processor.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log the IP addresses of visitors.

Pages load fonts, images and news headlines from third parties, for example Google Fonts and the rss2json.com news feed service. Those services see your IP address when your browser requests them.

Analytics and advertising

This site uses no analytics and no advertising trackers today. It may store your light or dark theme choice in your browser. It sets no tracking cookies.

If that changes, this page will be updated first.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, use the contact page.
